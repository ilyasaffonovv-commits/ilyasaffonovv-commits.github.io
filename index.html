TwistFrame Publisher

A private, internal automation tool used by the operator of the TwistFrame media brand to publish TwistFrame's own original short-form videos to TwistFrame's own social accounts.

Operated by: TwistFrame (YouTube @TwistFrameMedia, Instagram @twistframemedia, TikTok @twistframemedia)
Contact: safonov.creator@gmail.com

What it does

  • Takes a finished video file produced by TwistFrame.
  • Runs technical and content-quality checks.
  • Generates a title, description and hashtags tailored to each platform.
  • Uploads the video to TwistFrame's own YouTube channel, Instagram professional account and TikTok account through the official YouTube Data API v3, Instagram Content Publishing API and TikTok Content Posting API.
  • Confirms each upload and records the result.

Who can use it

Only the TwistFrame account owner, on their own machine, for their own accounts. It is not distributed, has no sign-up, no other users, and does not act on behalf of anyone else.

Data

The tool stores OAuth access tokens for TwistFrame's own accounts locally on the operator's computer, encrypted with the operating system's credential store. It does not collect data from any other person, shows no ads, and runs no analytics. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Privacy Policy

Last updated:

TwistFrame Publisher ("the tool") is a private automation tool operated by the owner of the TwistFrame media brand ("we", "us"). It runs on the operator's own computer and is used only to publish TwistFrame's own videos to TwistFrame's own social media accounts.

1. Information we process

  • OAuth tokens for the operator's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts, obtained when the operator authorizes the tool. These are used solely to upload videos and read back their status.
  • Video files and their metadata (titles, descriptions, hashtags) created by TwistFrame.
  • Publishing records — the IDs and URLs of videos the tool has uploaded, and any error messages, kept in a local database.

2. Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect personal information from any other person. The tool has no user accounts, no visitors, no contact forms, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. It does not read other users' content, followers, messages or profiles.

3. Where data is stored

All data stays on the operator's local computer. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest using the operating system's credential store (Windows DPAPI). Nothing is uploaded to us or to any third party other than the official platform APIs (YouTube, Instagram/Meta, TikTok) that are strictly required to publish a video the operator chose to publish.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data. Data is transmitted only to the official APIs of YouTube, Meta/Instagram and TikTok, and only to carry out an upload the operator initiated. Each of those platforms handles that data under its own privacy policy.

5. Retention and deletion

OAuth tokens are kept until the operator revokes them (in the platform's own settings) or deletes them from the tool. Local publishing records can be deleted by the operator at any time. To request deletion of anything related to this tool, contact safonov.creator@gmail.com.

6. Children

The tool is not directed to children and does not knowingly process any child's personal data.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
